About Us

Join a welcoming community of performers, creators, and theatre enthusiasts.

We are Nosham's home for everyone who loves the stage — whether you sing, act, write, build sets, or simply love watching a good show. The Music and Theatre Society brings together performers, creators, and theatre enthusiasts from across all campuses. We believe that musicals and plays are not just entertainment — they are a way to tell stories, build confidence, and create lasting friendships. Each semester, we run workshops (acting, vocal coaching, stage makeup, improv), social nights, play readings, and full-scale productions. No experience is required to join — only passion. Whether you want to be in the spotlight or help behind the scenes, you'll find a welcoming, supportive community here.

JOIN US

Welcom to Join US!!!

Not sure if theatre is for you? Everyone starts somewhere. At MTS, you don't need experience - just curiosity. Join us to perform, build sets, run lights, or simply make friends who love musicals and plays as much as you do.

Why Join Us?

Become part of a welcoming creative community where students can explore theatre, music, and performance in a supportive environment.
